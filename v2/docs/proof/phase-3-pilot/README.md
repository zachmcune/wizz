# Phase 3 pilot — art redo

**Status: awaiting Zachary's approval. This is a pilot, not the full roster.**

Scope is exactly what was asked for: 5 units (Wisp, Arcane Archer, Stone Golem, Imp Swarmling,
Storm Caster), 4 buildings (Sanctum, Mana Node, Stone Wall, a defence spire = Arcane Sentry), and one
terrain patch with a cliff and a ramp. The remaining 9 units and 17 buildings are **not** redone yet —
that is gated on this look being approved.

The renderer and pipeline from the previous pass are kept. What changed is how the art is *modelled*.

---

## What was wrong, and what actually caused it

Zachary's four points, and what each turned out to be in the code:

| Report | Cause |
|---|---|
| Units read as stacks of boxes/spheres | Every part was a sharp-edged box/cyl/sphere. No bevels, so nothing caught a highlight and every face read as a flat card; no ambient occlusion, so a stack of three boxes had no crease between them. |
| Buildings look broken, flat panels jutting out at odd angles | **Three separate bugs.** (1) `bbox` took `(w, h, d)` while `bevelBox` expected `(w, h, d)` but callers passed `(w, d, h)` — ~20 parts became horizontal slabs instead of vertical fins. (2) `prism` had `r1 = 0`, so every crystal, band and rune ring was silently built as a cone tapering to a point and vanished. (3) The rasteriser had **no depth buffer**, so backfaces painted over front faces. |
| Units are unreadable specks on flat purple terrain | Units drew at collision radius (~13 px at default zoom) on a single averaged purple colour. |
| Five proof images are byte-identical | The crop tool scrolled the page, then captured `clip: {x:0, y:0, ...}`. Playwright resolves `clip` in **document** coordinates, so the clip never followed the scroll and every capture grabbed the same top-left region. |

Two further bugs were found and fixed while doing this:

- **Every pilot sprite was invisible.** The atlas manifest writer omitted frame `h`, so each frame was
  `0` px tall — the whole roster drew as invisible slices, and only the drop shadows showed. The loader
  now throws on a zero-sized frame instead of silently drawing nothing.
- **Banners were laid flat.** `plate()` already builds an upright banner down the z axis; three call
  sites additionally applied `rotX: PI/2`, tipping the cloth horizontal so it read as a floating panel.

---

## Decision: 3D meshes, not 2D vectors

Justified in full in [`../../ART_DIRECTION.md`](../../ART_DIRECTION.md). The short version:

The game needs **8 facings per unit**. 14 units × 8 facings = **112 bespoke drawings** that all have
to stay consistent with each other in style, proportion and lighting. 3D geometry gives correct,
consistent facings for free and lets the effort go into *form* instead. Team colour also falls out of
the material system rather than being hand-masked 112 times.

Everything is still code-authored: every vertex comes from a function in `v2/art/`. **No generated
images anywhere in the pipeline.**

---

## What the new modelling actually does

- `art/mesh2.ts` — `bevelBox` (chamfered edges catch the specular), `tube` (sweeps a **per-section
  radius**, so limbs taper and bend at joints), `capsule`, `plate`, `prism`.
- `art/raster2.ts` — 4× supersampling, **14-ray voxel ambient occlusion** (56³ grid, cosine-weighted),
  rim light driven by the real camera direction, curvature-aware specular, wrap-around key light.
- `art/palette2.ts` — ~40 materials with per-material AO, specular, rim and grain.
- `art/pilot.ts` — each asset starts from a **silhouette plan**: the one shape that must survive at
  22 px, named in a comment before any part is placed.
- `src/render/terrain2.ts` — layered terrain: 6-tone noise-varied grass, dirt paths along the lanes,
  banded cliff strata with crack lines and a lit lip, packed-earth ramps with tread marks, and props
  (grass tufts, stones, mushrooms, ruin blocks).

**Readability at 667×375:** unit visual size is decoupled from collision size and drawn at **2.6×**
(`renderer.ts` `spriteScale`). Hit boxes are unchanged, so gameplay is identical — only the sprite is
bigger. A Stone Golem now spans ~34 world units ≈ **28 screen px** at default zoom.

## The proof pack

Everything here is regenerated by a script; none of it is hand-cropped.

### 1. Every pilot asset, 8 facings × 4 team colours
[`sprites/`](sprites/) — `wisp.png`, `arcane_archer.png`, `stone_golem.png`, `imp_swarmling.png`,
`storm_caster.png`, `sanctum.png`, `mana_node.png`, `stone_wall.png`, `arcane_sentry.png`.

Each sheet is sliced **out of the atlas manifest** using that entity's own frame key, and the caption
prints the key. Units have 8 facings; the four buildings bake one facing (a static structure does not
rotate), which the caption states explicitly.

### 2. v1 vs old v2 vs new
[`comparison-v1-oldv2-new.png`](comparison-v1-oldv2-new.png)

- **old v2** — frames from the committed previous-pass atlas (`v2/public/atlas/`).

### 3. In-match at default zoom, four viewports
[`match/`](match/) — `phone-667x375.png`, `phone-844x390.png`, `chromebook-1366x768.png`,
`desktop-1920x1080.png`, plus `match-report.json`.

20 units per side. Each shot is captured at `deviceScaleFactor: 1` (what a player actually sees) and
is **asserted to contain non-terrain pixels**, so an empty-terrain screenshot cannot be shipped as a
"massed battle" again.

### 4. Terrain patch with cliff and ramp
[`terrain/terrain-cliff-and-ramp.png`](terrain/terrain-cliff-and-ramp.png), plus `terrain-report.json`.

The site is chosen from the map data (largest height drop with a ramp tile nearby), not hard-coded.
Material coverage is measured against the real terrain palettes in `terrain2.ts` rather than guessed
from hue, and the report prints the mix.

## How the proof bug is prevented from recurring

`tests/pilot-proof.test.ts` (12 assertions) fails if:

- any two proof images share an md5;
- any proof still matches `297ca11317ed2539377f9317d55ea2de` (the hash of the five that were broken);
- a proof's atlas key names a **different entity** than the one it is filed under;
- a referenced frame has a zero or negative size (the invisible-sprite bug);
- a proof image is blank or has no horizontal extent;
- a pilot asset has no triangles, is degenerate, or is wildly off-centre;
- a pilot asset renders to an empty, too-dark or blown-out image.

Both guards were verified by deliberately breaking the data: pointing a proof at another entity, and
copying one sprite over another. The suite failed in each case, then passed again when restored.

## What is NOT proven here

- Only the 9 pilot assets are modelled to this standard. The other 26 entities still use the previous
  pass's art and will look inconsistent next to these until they are redone.
- No GPU exists in this environment (SwiftShader). Frame times in the debug overlay are CPU-side and
  are **not** a meaningful FPS prediction for a real device.
- The 8-facings-per-unit claim is proven for the 5 pilot units only.
