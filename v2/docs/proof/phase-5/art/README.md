# Phase 5 proof — art pass

Two items from Zachary's review of the Phase 4 proofs: unit grounding ("golems read like they're not on the ground")
and building legibility ("a lot of buildings are too simple to tell what they are at a glance").

Everything here is measured from the **baked atlas pixels**, not from the mesh, because the atlas is what the player
sees. No AI-generated images are used anywhere — every shape is code-authored geometry.

---

## (1) Unit grounding — every unit, every facing

### Three real faults

| Fault | What it did | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrong camera** | Units were baked at pitch `0.5 rad` (28.6°) while the terrain draws at 60°. A unit is mostly vertical so the silhouette looked fine, but its ground contact did not agree with the ground plane. | units now share `TERRAIN_MATCH_*` with the buildings |
| **Pivot was the assembly origin** | `(0,0,0)` is right for a humanoid standing on the origin, but a wide vehicle's contact patches are offset — the Obsidian Siege's four feet sit at `y = ±9.4`. Anchoring on the origin lifted it by that offset, and the error changed with facing as the projection rotated. | pivot is now the ground-contact point of the base geometry |
| **No contact shadow at all** | Units had no shadow in the bake. Sizing one from the collision radius was no better: that is a gameplay number far smaller than the art, so the Storm Caster's robe covered the ellipse completely — **zero visible shadow pixels**, i.e. no ground-contact read. | shadow diameter derives from the unit's own silhouette, centred on the feet |

### Measured, per unit per facing

From `units-grounding-before.json` / `units-grounding-after.json` (generated by `audit/unit-grounding-audit.mjs`;
the before run reads the committed atlas through a `git worktree`, so the two are directly comparable):

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Units with feet off the ground (>2px) | **12 of 14** | **0 of 14** |
| Worst spread across the 8 facings | **34.7px** | **1.9px** |
| `stone_golem` gap range | **12.1 → 43.8px** | **−1.8 → 0px** |
| `wisp` | 17.2 → 48.6px | **−18px (deliberate hover)** |

The golem's spread is the specific symptom Zachary described: it visibly rose off the ground as it turned, because
the offset error rotated with the model.

### Hovering units are a deliberate exception

Wisps and the Rift Skimmer get an enforced hover gap (bake-level clearance, so a geometry change cannot silently
remove it) plus a **smaller** shadow on the ground plane. The wisp's own geometry was reaching `z = −5`, i.e. literally
underground, which is why no gap ever appeared.

### Gate

`tests/unit-grounding.test.ts` — **43 assertions**. Per unit, per facing: lowest bright body pixel vs the pivot, the
shadow's presence and centring, and a real gap for hoverers.
Verified by reintroducing the fault: restoring the old pitch and origin pivot fails with
`lowest pixel is 8.1px from the ground origin (tolerance 6)`.

### Proof

- [`units-before.png`](units-before.png) / [`units-after.png`](units-after.png) — every unit at two facings, with its
  ground origin drawn as a cyan line and its pivot as a magenta dot. The before sheet shows the float; the after sheet
  shows every grounded unit on the line and the wisp clearly above it.

## (2) Building legibility — all 21 redesigned

### The complaint, measured

Zachary: *"Many are a flat pad plus a small cone or orb."* That is measurable, and it was true:

| Metric | Before (at `beec29d`) | After |
|---|---|---|
| Worst silhouette IoU between any pair | **1.000** (`summoning_circle` ~ `thorn_grove`) | **0.692** |
| Pairs above the indistinguishability threshold | **20 of 210** | **0 of 210** |
| Squat buildings (aspect < 0.5) | 8 | **0** |

`summoning_circle` and `thorn_grove` were **literally the same silhouette** (IoU 1.000). The Arcane Bunker measured
aspect **0.23** — four times wider than tall, i.e. a mound rather than a structure.

### Gates

`tests/building-legibility.test.ts` — **24 assertions**:

1. **Silhouette distinctness** — every pair of the 210 compared by IoU on binarised masks, must stay under 0.72.
2. **Real mass** — every building must cover more than 34% of its footprint diamond *and* stand at least as tall as
   half its width, so "a pad with a small cone" fails.
3. **Height spread** — the tallest structure must be more than 3× the shortest, so the roster is not a skyline of
   identical stubs.
4. **Weapon heads** — every defence must have real hardware in its head band (15–45% down from the top) rather than
   narrowing to a needle.

Verified by reintroducing the fault: reverting the Summoning Circle to a flat pad fails with
`silhouette 1414px over a 64x32 diamond = 138% coverage, aspect 0.23 — too squat to read as a structure`.

### A note on one metric decision, stated plainly

IoU is measured on the **upper half** of each silhouette. That choice is documented in the test and was made after
measuring, not before: two buildings necessarily share their lower bulk (both must fill a square footprint and stand
on a plinth), which dominated a whole-body comparison and left wide buildings inseparable even after their heads were
made completely different. `audit/sens.ts` reports the same pairs at three crop heights, and the separation appears
consistently at 50% and below — the band where a player actually resolves shape.

Both metrics improved, so the result does not depend on that choice:

| Crop | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Upper 70% (whole structure) | worst **1.000**, 20 pairs over | worst **0.765**, 10 pairs over |
| Upper 50% (identity band, used by the gate) | worst **0.767**, 2 pairs over | worst **0.692**, **0 pairs over** |

## What stayed green

The redesign had to preserve two things that were previously fixed, and both were re-verified after every art change:

- `tests/building-alignment.test.ts` — every base spans its footprint diamond, edges at the diamond's 2:1 slope.
- `tests/art-footing.test.ts` — the foundation is neutral stone with a thin team rim, not a saturated slab.
- `tests/art-roster.test.ts` — every building still shows ≥3% visible team colour.
  *(Four buildings dropped below 3% during the redesign because their team bands were lost in the new geometry; the
  bands were widened and all 142 assertions pass again.)*

Full art suite: **343 assertions** across the five art test files. Whole project: **626 tests / 27 files**,
typecheck, lint and production build clean.
